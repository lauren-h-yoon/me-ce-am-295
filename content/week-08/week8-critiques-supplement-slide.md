Reading the Claims Critically: GNoME & A-Lab Under Scrutiny

GNoME: 2.2M structures — how many are discoveries?

Cheetham & Seshadri, Chem. Mater. 36:3490 (2024) — Ref_22. Scant evidence of compounds that are strikingly novel, credible, AND useful; most predictions are minor variants of known materials.

A-Lab: 41 of 58 in 17 days — or 0 of 43?

Leeman et al., PRX Energy 3:011002 (2024) — Ref_23. Re-analysis of every claimed synthesis: automated XRD misidentified known phases; none was a genuinely new material.

The lesson is not that autonomous discovery failed — autonomous claims require autonomous-grade verification.
